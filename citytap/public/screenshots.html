🎮 CitySnipe Game Flow

Complete playthrough with screenshots - Daily #964

Step 1

Initial Game Screen

The game starts with a pin on the globe. You can rotate and zoom to explore the area and identify the city.

Step 2

City Search with Autocomplete

Type a city name and get instant suggestions. The search is smart and ranks by population - "Lond" immediately shows "London, United Kingdom".

Step 3

Round Results - Reveal Screen

After your guess, see how close you were! This shows the guess (London) vs. correct city (Linz, Austria), the distance (1089.7 km), and your score (10/100). Red square means far guess.

Step 4

Next Round

Continue to the next location. The previous pins remain visible on the globe so you can see where you've been.

Step 5

More Results

Round 3 result showing guess vs. correct city with distance calculation.

Step 6

Round 4 Results

Another round showing the scoring system in action.

Step 7

Final Round

Round 5 of 5 - the last location with the button now saying "View Final Results".

Step 8

Final Results Screen

CitySnipe #964 Complete! See your total score (50/500), all five rounds with emoji indicators (🟥), and detailed breakdown. Click "Share Results" to copy your score in emoji format!
